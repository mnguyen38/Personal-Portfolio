## Why I built it

[Your origin story in 2–3 sentences: what got you into options, and why you wanted your own tooling instead of an off-the-shelf platform.]

Most tools I found were built around macro bets on the index. I was more interested in **single-name setups**, where one company's news can move its options independently of the market, and I didn't want to trust any strategy until I'd tested it myself.

## How it works

1. **Backtest.** A strategy is replayed against historical market data first. [What data, what time range, how fills and costs are modelled.]
2. **Paper trade.** Strategies that pass graduate to an automated engine that trades through Alpaca's paper account, measuring implied volatility from live data and logging every decision to an activity log.
3. **Compete.** Two strategies, one single-name ("micro") and one macro, run side by side. A daily snapshot of each one's equity feeds the Sharpe ratio and return shown on this site.
4. **Watch.** A Next.js dashboard backed by PostgreSQL shows positions and sends push notifications to my phone.
