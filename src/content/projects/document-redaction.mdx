## How it works

The add-in runs inside Microsoft Word, on desktop and on the web. It reads the document's body, headers and tables through Office.js, and runs a detector for each of **13 kinds of sensitive data**: emails, phone numbers, social security numbers, card numbers, dates, street addresses, medical record numbers, account numbers, employee IDs, order numbers, licence numbers, insurance numbers and names.

You choose which types to redact from a checkbox panel. Matches are replaced with solid block characters, and a "CONFIDENTIAL DOCUMENT" header is added.
